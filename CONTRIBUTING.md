# Contributions are not accepted

This project is published so that you can read it, use it, and change your own copy. It is
maintained by one person, and **it does not take code from others.**

- **Pull requests are not reviewed or merged.** They are closed without comment on the code,
  whatever their quality. Please do not spend time preparing one.
- **Feature requests are not taken.** What is built next follows the maintainer's own use of
  the tool.
- **Bug reports are welcome** as [issues](https://github.com/adcident12/opencode-monitor/issues):
  what you did, what you saw, what you expected, your operating system, and the output of
  `node server.mjs --doctor`. A report is read; a fix, if one comes, is written by the
  maintainer. Please do not attach a patch.
- **Security problems:** open an issue that says only that you have found one, without the
  details, and you will be contacted.

## Why

Every change to this tool is checked by hand against a real OpenCode installation, and the
maintainer answers for all of it. Taking code from outside would mean reviewing and owning
work that cannot be checked that way. Saying so here is kinder than leaving a pull request
unanswered.

## What you are free to do

The code is under the [MIT licence](LICENSE). You may fork it, change it, and publish your
own version, without asking. If your fork goes its own way, please give it its own name, so
that nobody takes it for this project.
